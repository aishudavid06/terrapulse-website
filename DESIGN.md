## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an eco-modernist aesthetic engineered for environmental intelligence, planetary analytics, and civic action. Moving away from weathered, rustic tropes of legacy ecological branding, it treats sustainability with the technical rigor, clarity, and urgency of high-precision software.

The emotional tone balances scientific authority with optimism and decisive purpose. Surfaces feel airy and pristine, while deep arboreal tones anchor structural boundaries. High-contrast typography provides immediate legibility for dense real-time feeds, telemetry readouts, and communal initiatives.

Key visual attributes:
- **Pristine surfaces:** Clean white-tinted canvas backdrops that eliminate visual mud while reducing eye fatigue.
- **Architectural green hierarchies:** Dense forest tones establish framing, while vibrant leaf accents draw eyes to kinetic metrics and actionable pathways.
- **Tactile data surfaces:** Subtly framed metric containers that read like scientific glass slides or precision sensors.

## Colors

The color architecture reflects a synthesis of botanical depth, active biosignals, and clinical utility:

- **Primary (`#0F382A` - Deep Forest):** Used for primary CTAs, high-level structural headers, active navigation states, and authoritative visual anchors.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Vibrant Emerald):** Signifies live telemetry, positive environmental impact metrics, validated goals, progress trajectories, and interactive toggles.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Solar Amber):** Reserved specifically for alerts, risk thresholds, critical atmospheric alerts, urgent calls to action, and spotlight badges.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Slate Charcoal):** Primary text and iconography color, ensuring crisp contrast against light substrates without the jarring harshness of pure black.
- **Canvas & Surface Tint (`#F7FAF7` - Crisp Bio-Cream):** The default application canvas, evoking bleached organic linen and minimizing screen glare.
- **Border & Structure Accent (`#E2E8F0` - Cool Slate):** Provides hairline separation between dense data modules, metric cells, and surface panels.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances modern humanist clarity with geometric structure:

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** leads all display headings, section titling, and numeric metric callouts. Its soft geometric curves grant an approachable, clean aura without losing authoritative presence.
- **Inter** handles body text, tabular data displays, metadata labels, and form controls. Its neutral execution guarantees optimal optical legibility across complex environmental telemetry and long-form action briefs.
- Numeric indicators and sensor outputs lean heavily on tabular spacing where necessary, utilizing negative letter-spacing for large-scale displays to reinforce an engineered feel.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to manage varying content forms—from real-time sensor dashboards to dense petition directories and community news loops.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (24px) gutter, 3rem (48px) margin. Max-width container at 1440px.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem (20px) gutter, 2rem (32px) margin.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (16px) gutter, 1rem (16px) margin.

- **Rhythm & Flow:**
  - Component padding strictly scales in increments of 4px and 8px.
  - Section-level dividers utilize `space-xl` for breathing room, keeping analytical data digestible.
  - Card collections leverage `space-md` gaps on desktop, shifting down to `space-sm` for stacked data strips on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy physical drop shadows in favor of a crisp, architectural layering system rooted in tonal containment and low-contrast outlines:

- **Surface Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#F7FAF7`. Elevated interactive cards, data panels, and toolbars sit on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Every card, container, and elevated panel is wrapped with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`, establishing boundary precision without visual friction.
- **Tonal Shadows:** When floating overlays, dialogs, or active popovers demand elevation, shadows use deep green-tinted ambient diffusion:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 56, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 56, 42, 0.04)`
- **Hover Transitions:** Interactive cards lift subtly via a 1px vertical translation combined with a slight border shift toward `#CBD5E1`, preserving minimal, intentional feedback.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced geometry characterized by structured confidence softened by rounded contours:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, utility dropdowns, list rows, and inner metric blocks.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px):** Standard for cards, metric dashboards, action modules, and alert sheets.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Reserved for major hero containers, modal dialogs, and feature spotlight frames.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Used exclusively for status badges, tags, category pills, filter chips, and primary micro-buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Forest (`#0F382A`) background with white text. Rounded corners (`rounded-lg` / 1rem). Height 44px (desktop) / 48px (mobile). Subtly scales down opacity on hover; transitions into a dark emerald tone.
- **Secondary / Action:** Solid Emerald (`#10B981`) background with white text. Used for direct environmental actions like "Join Clean-up", "Donate Solar", or "Sign Petition".
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), charcoal text (`#1E293B`). Shifts to `#F7FAF7` on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- Designed as full pills (`rounded-full`).
- **Telemetry / Status Pill:** Emerald-tinted background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`) paired with `#0F382A` or `#10B981` text, flanked by an active 6px green indicator beacon.
- **Warning / Critical Pill:** Solar Amber tint (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`) with `#B45309` text for urgent air quality alerts or critical climate warnings.
- **Filter Chips:** 1px `#E2E8F0` border with body-sm text; selected state fills with `#0F382A` and switches text to white.

### Metric & Telemetry Cards
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px `#E2E8F0` outline and `rounded-lg` corners.
- Internal padding set to `space-lg`.
- Top-level label in `label-md` uppercase muted slate, followed by a bold numeric read in `metric-display`, with secondary delta trends (e.g., "+12.4% vs last week") encapsulated in an emerald pill.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Borderless rows separated by 1px cool slate lines (`#E2E8F0`).
- Left-aligned ecological iconography housed in subtle rounded squares (`#F7FAF7` background), paired with two-line body text: title in `label-lg` charcoal, metadata in `body-sm` muted slate.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp white canvas, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), `rounded-md` radius. Focus ring states introduce a 2px outer ring in `#10B981` with zero drop shadow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Emerald active fill (`#10B981`) with white check icon. Inactive states feature `#E2E8F0` border against white surface with smooth transition timing.
- **Toggles:** Pill-shaped rail with neutral off-state (`#E2E8F0`) and energetic emerald on-state (`#10B981`).

### Alert Strips
- Prominent banners highlighting regional alerts (e.g., "Air Quality Advisory").
- Pale amber background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`), left accent border of 4px solid `#F59E0B`, paired with a bold amber icon and concise markdown resolution steps.
